# UMKM Ketapangtelu

A product catalog website for the local small businesses (UMKM) of Ketapangtelu Village, developed as a community service (KKN) project in the 6th semester.

**Website:** [umkm-ketapangtelu.vercel.app](https://umkm-ketapangtelu.vercel.app/)

## About

This website helps local business owners in Ketapangtelu Village promote their products online, including smoked fish, fish crackers, shrimp paste (terasi jembret), banana chips, seblak, and catering packages.

## Features

- Product catalog with descriptions and prices
- Products grouped by business owner
- Product detail pages
- About and Contact pages
- Responsive design for desktop and mobile
